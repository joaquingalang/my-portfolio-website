# Design Context

## Product
Personal portfolio site for Joaquin ("Quin") Galang — full-stack developer (Flutter, React,
Firebase, Node.js), based in Pampanga, Philippines. Single-page, scroll-driven.

## Target audience
Two groups, weighted equally:

1. **Clients / freelance leads** — want to see outcomes and shipped work, then contact him.
2. **Recruiters / hiring managers** — scan fast, want credibility signals (awards, community
   leadership) and a frictionless path to the CV.

Both audiences skim before they read. Neither is here to admire effects.

## Use cases
- Scan the work in under 30 seconds and judge whether he's worth contacting.
- Grab the CV.
- Verify credibility (awards, community roles, real shipped links).
- Send a message.

## Brand personality / tone
Confident and technical without being cold. Precise, not flashy. The site should feel like it
was **built by someone who sweats details** — the craft of the site is itself a portfolio piece.
Restrained motion; the content is the star.

## Visual identity (locked — refine, do not replace)
- **Base** `#0B0B0D` near-black. **Surface** `#141515`. **Elevated surface** `#1B1D1C`.
- **Accent** `#60e08e` mint. Used sparingly: eyebrows, focus, active state, one primary CTA.
- **Secondary accent** `#45FFCA` — reserve for rare emphasis.
- **Text** `#F6F7FF` at 100 / 70 / 45% opacity for primary / secondary / tertiary.
- **Type**: Sarpanch (display, h1), Chakra Petch (section headings, labels), Poppins (body).

## Quality bar
Flagship. This is the artifact that represents his craft — details are the point.

## Motion principles
- Purposeful only: entrances, state changes, feedback.
- `ease-out-quart` / `ease-out-expo`. Never bounce or elastic.
- Animate `transform` and `opacity` only.
- Every animation must have a `prefers-reduced-motion` path.

## Non-negotiables
- WCAG AA contrast on all text.
- Visible focus indicator on every interactive element.
- 44×44px minimum touch targets.
- No layout shift on load.
